# KPI Framework

Status: Canonical  
Effective: 2026-09-21

## North-Star Chain

**Influence → Opportunity → Revenue**

Follower Count به‌تنهایی KPI راهبردی نیست.

## KPI Layers

### 1. Attention Quality
- Reach
- Completion / Read depth where available
- Video retention
- Save rate
- Share rate

### 2. Audience Quality
- Qualified followers
- C-level / Founder follower growth
- Geographic expansion
- International audience share

### 3. Conversation Quality
- Meaningful comments
- Decision-maker comments
- Qualified DMs
- Repeated commenters / community

### 4. Authority
- Brand search
- AI citations / mentions
- Media invitations
- Podcast invitations
- Speaking invitations
- Backlinks / citations
- Newsletter subscribers

### 5. Opportunity
- Qualified leads
- Strategic partnerships
- Talent inbound
- Investor inbound
- Enterprise introductions

### 6. Business Impact
- Influenced pipeline
- Revenue influenced
- Partner revenue
- Hiring impact
- PR value where measurable

## Content-Level Review

هر محتوا پس از انتشار باید حداقل با این موارد ارزیابی شود:
- هدف محتوا
- مخاطب هدف
- Evidence type
- Distribution
- Quality response
- Business signal
- Learning

هدف، ساخت Loop یادگیری است؛ نه مسابقه Vanity Metric.
